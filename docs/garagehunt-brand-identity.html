Concept

Rooted in the garage sale itself

The signature comes from what a garage sale actually looks like: hand-written price tags taped to furniture, a folding table, a marker sign at the end of the driveway. The whole identity is built around the price tag as a shape and Saturday-morning energy as a mood — not a generic "friendly startup" look.

Palette

Sale-sign colors, not tech-startup colors

Warm paper background instead of a cold white app canvas. Marigold and coral do the energetic lifting; deep plum grounds it instead of the usual near-black.

Signature element

The price tag

Every status badge and category chip is shaped like a price tag — squared on one side, rounded on the other, with a punched hole. It's the one motif repeated everywhere so the app is recognizable even in a screenshot with no logo visible.
